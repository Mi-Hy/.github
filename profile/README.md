# Mi-Hy Open Research & Development Ecosystem

Welcome to the official GitHub organisation of the [Mi-Hy Project](https://www.mi-hy.eu/), a Horizon Europe EIC Pathfinder project exploring next-generation microbial hydroponics, bioelectrochemical systems, and regenerative urban agriculture.

This organisation hosts open-source software, hardware designs, datasets, experimental tools, educational material, and reproducible research artefacts developed within the Mi-Hy ecosystem.

---

## About Mi-Hy

Mi-Hy (*Microbial Hydroponics: Circular Sustainable Electrobiosynthesis*) combines:

- Microbial Fuel Cells (MFCs)
- Microbial Electrolysis Cells (MECs)
- Hydroponic cultivation
- Wastewater valorisation
- Low-power bioelectronic systems
- Urban agriculture and regenerative design

The project introduces the concept of a *prosthetic rhizosphere*, extending plant root microbiomes within hydroponic systems to improve nutrient uptake, reclaim nitrogen and phosphorus from wastewater, and generate electricity from microbial activity.

Mi-Hy aims to develop circular, decentralised, low-power infrastructures that connect food production, energy generation, wastewater treatment, microbial ecosystems, and digital technologies.

## Interdisciplinary Vision

Mi-Hy operates at the intersection of:

- Biotechnology
- Bioelectronics
- Environmental engineering
- Architecture and design
- Sustainable agriculture
- Circular economy
- Urban systems
- Low-power digital infrastructure

Beyond technical innovation, the project explores how biological systems, design, and technology can jointly contribute to resilient and sustainable urban environments.
